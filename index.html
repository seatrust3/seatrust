About

Seatrust is a maritime brand established in 1999, now evolving into a Web3-based trust and identity layer for the global shipping and marine industry.

What We Do

  • Maritime Digital Identity for vessels & companies
  • Blockchain-based verification of documents & certifications
  • Secure, tamper-proof maritime records

How It Works

  1. Connect identity via blockchain domain (ENS)
  2. Register vessel or company profile
  3. Verify documents and credentials
  4. Create trusted maritime presence on-chain

Use Cases

Shipping companies • Port operators • Maritime insurance • Logistics verification
