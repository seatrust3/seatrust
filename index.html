About Seatrust

Seatrust is a maritime trust and identity initiative established in 1999, originally operating in the maritime and shipping domain with early digital presence during the first generation of the web.

Today, Seatrust is evolving into a modern Web3 maritime trust layer, focused on digital identity, verification, and secure representation for global shipping operations.

By combining decades of maritime experience with decentralized technologies, Seatrust bridges traditional maritime operations with the emerging world of blockchain-based identity systems.
